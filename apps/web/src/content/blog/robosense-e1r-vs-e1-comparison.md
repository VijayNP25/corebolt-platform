## Introduction

RoboSense’s E1R and E1 LiDARs are designed for different sensing priorities—robotics vs. automotive. Understanding their strengths helps developers choose the right sensor for perception, navigation, or ADAS applications.

## RoboSense E1R – Overview

The RoboSense **E1R** is a compact, fully solid-state LiDAR built for high-precision, real-time perception.

### Key Features
- Wide 120° × 90° field of view  
- High-density point cloud using SPAD-SoC + VCSEL  
- Max range up to 75 m  
- Excellent data rate with dual-return modes 
- Automotive-grade durability (IP67 / IP6K9K)  
- Low power consumption and compact footprint

The E1R is ideal for **robots, AGVs, warehouse automation, AMRs, delivery robots,** and outdoor-capable platforms.

## RoboSense E1 – Overview

The [**RoboSense E1**](https://www.core-bolt.com/lidar/robosense/robosense-e1-lidar-sensor) is a flash-solid-state LiDAR optimized for **automotive blind-spot detection** and large-scale OEM deployments.

### Key Highlights
- 120° × 90° ultra-wide FoV  
- Simple flash architecture with high durability
- Ultra-high frame rate (>25 Hz)  
- Designed for large OEM-level automotive integration
- Optimized performance for short-range sensing

The E1 is perfect for **ADAS systems, corner/blind-spot perception and high-volume automotive fleets**.

## RoboSense LiDAR Comparison — E1R vs E1

| Feature | RoboSense E1R | RoboSense E1 |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| Scanning Type | Digital solid-state (SPAD-SoC + VCSEL) | Flash solid-state |
| Field of View | 120° H × 90° V | 120° H × 90° V |
| Maximum Range | Up to 75 m | ~30 m |
| Point Cloud Density | High (~520k pts/s dual return) | Moderate–High (~810k pts/s depending on mode) |
| Frame Rate | ~10 Hz | 25 Hz+ |
| Accuracy | ±3 cm | ±5 cm |
| Ingress Protection | IP67 & IP6K9K | Automotive grade |
| Use-Case Fit | Robotics, mapping,outdoor mobile robots | Automotive blind-spot, ADAS, large fleets |

This **RoboSense LiDAR comparison** shows the distinct priorities of each sensor:
- **E1R = High accuracy + range + point cloud density.**
- **E1 = High frame rate + automotive scaling.**
