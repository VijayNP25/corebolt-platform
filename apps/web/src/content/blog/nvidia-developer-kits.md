## What Are NVIDIA Developer Kits?

**NVIDIA Developer Kits** are complete development platforms that combine high-performance computing modules with developer-friendly hardware interfaces. Each kit includes a **Jetson System-on-Module (SoM)** mounted on a carrier board with rich connectivity options — making it ideal for **robotics, computer vision, autonomous systems, and industrial AI.**

These kits are powered by NVIDIA’s advanced AI stack — including CUDA, TensorRT, DeepStream, and JetPack SDK — enabling real-time inference and deployment at the edge.
